I build safety certificates for autonomous systems and make them checkable by machine. My work synthesizes barrier and Lyapunov certificates with sum-of-squares (SOS) programming, chains them into hierarchies that scale to harder systems, and repairs the solver's floating-point output into exact certificates verified in Lean 4. I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Colorado Boulder, advised by Prof. [Sriram Sankaranarayanan](https://home.cs.colorado.edu/~srirams/), and collaborate with Toyota Research North America. You can try interactive versions of each project on my [research page](/research/).

Before my Ph.D., I spent over a decade in aerospace simulation and autonomous systems, including acceptance testing of fielded systems, and taught avionics and control engineering at NUST. That background shapes what I care about: guarantees that hold up on real hardware, and explanations that engineers and students can follow.

**I am looking for research scientist and postdoctoral positions** in formal verification for autonomous and learning-enabled systems. Feel free to reach out by email.

Outside work, I spend time with my family in [Boulder](https://www.bouldercoloradousa.com/) and on the tennis court whenever I can.
